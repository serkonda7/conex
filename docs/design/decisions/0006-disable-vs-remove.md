# 0006: Disabling is the off switch, removing lives in a danger zone
- Date: 2026-11-19
- Status: accepted

Replaces the general parts of the former 0005 (integrations), which was a page
design rather than a convention.

Objects that can be switched off and deleted (integrations, sync jobs, …):

- **Disable** is the everyday off switch. It sits on the object's detail page
  (an **Enabled** switch that saves at once) and keeps all data.
- **Remove** is only offered in a **danger zone** at the end of the object's
  settings, never on a card, in a list row or in a ⋯ menu. The danger zone says
  what removing deletes and what it keeps, then shows the Remove button.

Settings of such an object are a **Settings** tab of its detail page, placed
last, with one Save for everything edited rather than switched. There is no
separate edit page.
